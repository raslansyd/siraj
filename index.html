Gate Receiving & 3-Way PO Cross-Check

When supplier delivery vehicles arrive at the hypermarket receiving docks, Siraj cross-examines the driver's Delivery Note against the authorized Purchase Order (PO) in the ERP. Only pre-authorized items and confirmed delivery dates are admitted to the receiving staging zone.

Key Controls: Check PO validity, verify supplier license, cross-check delivery timestamps.
Systems / Tools: SAP B1 / S/4 HANA PO Module, Inward Logbook, Dock Scanners.
Business Impact: Eliminates unauthorized shipments, wrong vendor consignments, and over-delivery liability.
